# 08 · Bridge Builder

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m8">
  <Module.Question>
    How can a robot build a bridge in stages to connect two communities?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    Students place bridge sections inspired by the need to connect areas separated by rivers,
    ravines, or difficult roads. The module works with transmission, color as a reference, arm
    positions, and incremental construction.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Staged construction",
    "Color sensor",
    "Bevel-gear transmission",
    "Manipulator positions",
    "Progress measurement",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 12 min" title="Bridges in the community">
    Talk about rivers, ravines, traffic, and connecting places. What makes a bridge important?
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 25 min" title="Traction and arm">
    Observe the 4x4 transmission, drive motor, color sensor, and arm. Identify low, medium, and
    high positions.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 90-120 min" title="Place sections">
    Build the Bridge Builder. Measure progress by rotation, detect start/end with color, place a
    section, and go back for another.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="Infrastructure and planning">
    Discuss why building in stages requires clear references, measurement, and repeatability.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Cnb items={[
    "Social and Natural Environment: infrastructure, community, and mobility.",
    "Mathematics: measurement, units, comparison, and proportionality.",
    "Natural Sciences: mechanisms and transmission.",
    "Productivity and Development: planning technical processes.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Standards items={[

  ]} />

  <Module.Evaluation items={[
    "Measures distance by rotation.",
    "Uses arm positions for a real task.",
    "Explains how color serves as a construction reference.",
  ]} />
</Module>
