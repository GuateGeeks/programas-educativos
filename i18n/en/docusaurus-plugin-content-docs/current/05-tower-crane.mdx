# 05 · Tower Crane

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m5">
  <Module.Question>
    How does a crane move a heavy load with precision and balance on a job site in our city?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    Students build a GuateGeeks construction crane. The module combines pulley mechanics, a winch,
    motor positions, manual control, and autonomous sensor-based operation.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Fixed and movable pulleys",
    "Winch and rope",
    "Angular position",
    "Manual/autonomous control",
    "Balance and load",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 15 min" title="Building at height">
    Ask what machines students have seen on construction sites. What would happen if a crane moved
    without precision?
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 30 min" title="Pulleys and load">
    Compare fixed and movable pulleys. Observe how the winch spools the rope and how the arm turns.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 90-120 min" title="Move loads between points">
    Build the Tower Crane. Program raising/lowering the load, turning the arm, and automate a
    transfer using measured positions.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="Force, balance, and safety">
    Discuss why speed isn't always the goal. The crane must be stable, predictable, and safe.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Cnb items={[
    "Natural Sciences: force, simple machines, pulleys, and balance.",
    "Mathematics: degrees, measurement, and proportionality.",
    "Productivity and Development: safety in construction processes.",
    "Communication and Language: oral technical explanation.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Standards items={[
    "NGSS: simple machines and engineering design.",
    "CSTA: events and variables.",
    "ISTE: systematic testing of solutions.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Evaluation items={[
    "Relates pulleys to effort reduction.",
    "Uses motor positions to control the load.",
    "Prioritizes safety in the program's design.",
  ]} />
</Module>
