# 11 · Fire Rescue Unit

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m11">
  <Module.Question>
    How can a robot help in an emergency without putting a person in danger?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    The Fire Rescue Unit enters a house, senses distance, raises an arm with a color sensor to
    locate fire, and carries out a neutralizing or rescue action. The module connects technology
    with prevention and community care.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Emergency robotics",
    "Distance sensor",
    "Parallelogram arm",
    "Color detection",
    "Shortest path and rescue",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 15 min" title="Emergencies and safety">
    Locate the nearest fire extinguisher in the classroom. Talk about why robots don't replace
    firefighters, but do reduce risk.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 30 min" title="Entry, search, and arm">
    Review the ultrasonic sensor, lifting arm, and color sensor. Test height levels and flame
    detection.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 90-120 min" title="Search and neutralize">
    Build the Fire Rescue Unit. Program entry up to a distance, color search, stopping the arm at
    the flame, and executing an extinguish/rescue action.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="Technology with human purpose">
    Each group identifies a dangerous task in their own community where a robot could help.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Cnb items={[
    "Natural Sciences: risk prevention, fire, safety, and technology.",
    "Civic Education: care for life and community.",
    "Mathematics: height, distance, and comparison.",
    "Communication: presenting an emergency solution.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Standards items={[
    "NGSS ETS: designing safe solutions.",
    "CSTA: sensors, conditions, and procedures.",
    "ISTE: technology for social impact.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Evaluation items={[
    "Integrates distance and color into one mission.",
    "Sequences search, detection, and action.",
    "Explains the robot's social value.",
  ]} />
</Module>
