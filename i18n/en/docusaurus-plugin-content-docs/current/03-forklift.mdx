# 03 · Forklift

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m3">
  <Module.Question>
    How does a robot move a load in a market, warehouse, or workshop without crashing or losing
    precision?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    Students build a forklift inspired by local warehouses, markets, and workshops. The module
    works with centimeter-level movement, controlled turns, motorized lifting, and repeatable
    maneuvers.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Maneuverability",
    "Distance-based movement",
    "Rack and pinion",
    "Load lifting",
    "Precision testing",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 12 min" title="Warehouses and logistics">
    Show images or describe a supermarket/warehouse. Why does a forklift need precision?
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 25 min" title="Chassis and forks">
    Identify the drive motors and the manipulator motor. Review how the rack converts rotation into
    vertical movement.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 70-100 min" title="Pick up and place a container">
    Build the Forklift. Program a measured approach, raise the forks, approach the container, turn,
    and place it in a marked zone.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="Precision isn't luck">
    Measure how far the load ended up from the target. Build a table of attempt, error, and
    adjustment.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Cnb items={[
    "Mathematics: measurement, estimation, angles, and data logging.",
    "Natural Sciences: motion transfer and mechanisms.",
    "Communication and Language: arguing for technical decisions.",
    "Productivity and Development: work processes, logistics, and efficiency.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Standards items={[
    "CSTA: testing and debugging.",
    "ISTE: computational thinking.",
    "NGSS ETS: optimization through testing.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Evaluation items={[
    "Programs measured, repeatable movements.",
    "Recognizes the relationship between mechanism and function.",
    "Uses test data to improve.",
  ]} />
</Module>
