# 01 · Mapper Bot

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m1">
  <Module.Question>
    How can a robot draw a neighborhood's blueprint by following exact instructions?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    Students build a GuateGeeks mapping robot that draws on paper. The challenge connects
    neighborhood maps — streets, houses, markets, and services — with distance-based movement, 90°
    and 45° turns, loops, and physically calibrating the marker.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Sequential algorithm",
    "Distance via wheel rotation",
    "90° and 45° turns",
    "Loops for geometric shapes",
    "Calibration and testing",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 15 min" title="City blueprints">
    Show a simple map of the community. Ask what blueprints are for and what information is worth
    showing or leaving out.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 25 min" title="Designing the drawing robot">
    Identify two motors, a distance sensor, wheels, a caster ball, and a marker. Discuss why the
    marker must sit at the center of the turning axis.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 60-90 min" title="Build, program, and calibrate">
    Build the Mapper Bot using the guide. Download the base program. Program it to move forward
    5 cm, turn 90°, repeat 4 times, then draw a house with a roof.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="From error to precision">
    Compare drawings between teams. Identify whether the issue was mechanical, mathematical, or in
    the program. Record which adjustment improved precision the most.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Cnb items={[
    "Mathematics: measurement, geometry, estimation, and the relationship between distance and rotation.",
    "Natural Sciences: machines, motion, force, and cause-effect observation.",
    "Communication and Language: orally explaining the algorithm and the adjustments made.",
    "Productivity and Development: using technology to represent and address needs in the environment.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Standards items={[
    "CSTA: algorithms, sequences, and loops.",
    "ISTE: innovative design and debugging.",
    "NGSS ETS: define, test, and improve a solution.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Evaluation items={[
    "Calculates or adjusts distance and turns with evidence.",
    "Uses loops to simplify the program.",
    "Explains how the marker's position affects the result.",
  ]} />
</Module>
