# 11 · Fire Rescue Unit

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m11">
  <Module.Question>
    How can a robot help in an emergency without putting a person in danger?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    The Fire Rescue Unit enters a house, senses distance, raises an arm with a color sensor to
    locate fire, and carries out a neutralizing or rescue action. The module connects technology
    with prevention and community care.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Emergency robotics",
    "Distance sensor",
    "Parallelogram arm",
    "Color detection",
    "Shortest path and rescue",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 15 min" title="Emergencies and safety">
    Locate the nearest fire extinguisher in the classroom and make clear that the mission uses a
    simulated emergency, with no real fire or hazardous materials. Each team defines entry, search
    zone, and rescue or neutralizing action. Initial evidence: route sketch with safe distance and
    one reason to use a robot as support, not a replacement, for trained people.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 30 min" title="Entry, search, and arm">
    Review the ultrasonic sensor, lifting arm, and color sensor. Test distance readings and height
    levels while the arm moves slowly, keeping hands away from the mechanism. Checkpoint: the team
    identifies the stopping distance, the height that detects the simulated flame, and which reading
    could be false.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 90-120 min" title="Search and neutralize">
    Build the Fire Rescue Unit and program the mission as states: enter up to a distance, raise the
    arm, search for color, stop at the simulated flame, and execute an extinguish or rescue action.
    Record detected distance, color read, arm height, action, and failure point. If it fails, debug
    minimum distance, lighting, sensor height, entry route, or neutralizing timing.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="Technology with human purpose">
    Each group identifies a dangerous task in its community where a robot could help and explains
    which decisions would still need to be human. Submit brief evidence: rescue log and one safety
    improvement. To scale the challenge, 1st básico identifies sensors, 2nd básico tunes search data,
    and 3rd básico optimizes the route and argues the system's ethical limits.
  </Module.Phase>
