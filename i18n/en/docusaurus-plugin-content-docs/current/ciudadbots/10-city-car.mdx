# 10 · City Car

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m10">
  <Module.Question>
    How can a vehicle decide its route by reading signals from a city's environment?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    The City Car uses color markers to turn, move forward, head to a charging station, or stop. It
    integrates a color sensor, messages, and orientation to imagine cleaner, more orderly urban
    mobility.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Autonomous navigation",
    "Color markers",
    "Messages",
    "Yaw-based turns",
    "Urban route design",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 15 min" title="Cars and traffic">
    Discuss traffic, small vehicles, clean energy, and signals. Each team designs a short circuit
    with a lane, charging station, stop, and two turns, leaving space to retrieve the robot only when
    it is stopped. Initial evidence: route map with colors and a criterion for avoiding collisions.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 25 min" title="Markers and decisions">
    Assign meaning to colors: blue right, purple left, green charge, red stop. Take readings before
    the route, check spacing between markers, and test one action per color with only one robot in
    the lane. Checkpoint: the team completes a color-action table and explains what the robot should
    do if the sensor reads an unexpected color.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 90-120 min" title="Urban circuit">
    Build the City Car, create messages for each color, and program continuous movement, detection,
    and the corresponding action. Test individual segments first and then the full circuit,
    recording marker read, message sent, action executed, and result. If it fails, debug lighting,
    marker spacing, yaw turn, wrong message, or drive speed.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="Communication between vehicles">
    Imagine how several City Cars would share information to avoid collisions and propose a simple
    protocol: priority, stop, occupied charging station, or clear route. Each group submits one
    signaling improvement based on its route log. To scale the challenge, 1st básico follows a
    simple route, 2nd básico compares two routes with data, and 3rd básico designs rules for
    multiple vehicles.
  </Module.Phase>
