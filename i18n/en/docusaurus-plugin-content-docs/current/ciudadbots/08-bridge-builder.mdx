# 08 · Bridge Builder

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m8">
  <Module.Question>
    How can a robot build a bridge in stages to connect two communities?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    Students place bridge sections inspired by the need to connect areas separated by rivers,
    ravines, or difficult roads. The module works with transmission, color as a reference, arm
    positions, and incremental construction.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Staged construction",
    "Color sensor",
    "Bevel-gear transmission",
    "Manipulator positions",
    "Progress measurement",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 12 min" title="Bridges in the community">
    Talk about rivers, ravines, traffic, and connecting places. Each team divides the bridge into
    sections and decides which reference will mark the start, placement point, and return for another
    piece. Initial evidence: a drawn staged-construction sequence with an alignment criterion for
    accepting a section.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 25 min" title="Traction and arm">
    Observe the 4x4 transmission, drive motor, color sensor, and arm. Test low, medium, and high
    positions with a light section, keeping the path clear before moving the robot. Checkpoint: the
    team explains how the color mark starts or stops a stage and which arm height prevents dragging
    the piece.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 90-120 min" title="Place sections">
    Build the Bridge Builder and program one complete stage: measure progress by rotation, detect
    start or end with color, place a section, and return for another. Record a photo or sketch of
    each placement with distance to the edge and alignment between sections. If it fails, debug the
    color mark, floor traction, arm height, or travel distance before adding more stages.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="Infrastructure and planning">
    Discuss why building in stages requires clear references, measurement, and repeatability. Each
    team identifies one decision that made the bridge more stable or better aligned. To scale the
    challenge, 1st básico places one guided section, 2nd básico measures alignment between sections,
    and 3rd básico redesigns the sequence to improve repeatability or save movements.
  </Module.Phase>

    "Measures distance by rotation and verifies whether each section lands within the expected zone.",
