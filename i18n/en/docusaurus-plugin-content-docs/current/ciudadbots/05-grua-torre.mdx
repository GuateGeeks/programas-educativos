# 05 · Tower Crane

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m5">
  <Module.Question>
    How does a crane move a heavy load with precision and balance on a job site in our city?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    Students build a GuateGeeks construction crane. The module combines pulley mechanics, a winch,
    motor positions, manual control, and autonomous sensor-based operation.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Fixed and movable pulleys",
    "Winch and rope",
    "Angular position",
    "Manual/autonomous control",
    "Balance and load",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 15 min" title="Building at height">
    Ask what machines students have seen on construction sites and draw a safe zone around the
    load. Each team defines the origin point, destination point, and speed limit for moving without
    excessive swing. Initial evidence: a transfer sketch with a clear area and one safety rule for
    not touching the load while it is suspended.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 30 min" title="Pulleys and load">
    Compare fixed and movable pulleys, observe how the winch spools the rope, and test arm rotation
    without a load. Then use a light load to detect swing and check that the base does not lift.
    Checkpoint: the team identifies which motor lifts, which motor turns, and what they would do if
    the rope crosses itself or the load starts oscillating.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 90-120 min" title="Move loads between points">
    Build the Tower Crane and program the mission in stages: raise the load, turn to a measured
    position, lower it, and release or mark arrival. Record height, angle, stability, and result for
    each attempt in a safe-load log. If it fails, debug rope spooling, load weight, unstable base,
    motor speed, or angular position before repeating.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="Force, balance, and safety">
    Discuss why speed is not always the goal: the crane must be stable, predictable, and safe. Each
    group explains one decision that reduced risk and one measurement that improved precision. To
    scale the challenge, 1st básico observes pulley action and swing, 2nd básico compares
    angles/heights, and 3rd básico proposes automatic limits for safe operation.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Cnb items={[

    "Mathematics: motor degrees, measuring height/turn, proportionality, and precision records during transfer.",
    "Learning and Communication Technologies: using motor variables or positions, testing sequences, and documenting safe load handling.",
    "Communication and Language, Spanish: oral technical explanation using vocabulary for pulley, winch, balance, and constraint.",
    "Entrepreneurship for Productivity: safety, quality, and continuous improvement in an automated construction process.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Standards items={[
    "ISTE 1.4 and 1.5: tests an engineering solution while considering safety and control constraints.",
    "2026 CSTA PK-12 · Middle school: position variables/data, events, reading code, and test-based refinement.",
    "NGSS MS-ETS1-1 and MS-ETS1-3: defines safe-load criteria and analyzes test data to improve the crane.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Evaluation items={[
    "Relates pulleys and winch to effort reduction or load control using observable evidence.",
    "Uses motor positions to lift, lower, and turn the load without abrupt movements.",
    "Records tests of precision, stability, or time and explains which adjustment improved the result.",
    "Prioritizes one safety constraint in the program and justifies how it is verified.",
  ]} />
</Module>
