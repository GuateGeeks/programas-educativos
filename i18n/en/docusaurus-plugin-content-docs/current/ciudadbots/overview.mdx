experience into the classroom: implementation, tracking, and evaluation all in one place.

## How to use this material

- **Browse module by module.** Each module organizes implementation, resources, CNB alignment, and
  teacher evaluation in one place.
- **What's included.** Base LEGO SPIKE programs, a build-guide viewer, methodology guidance, and
  ready-to-use rubrics.
- **How to plan.** A flexible track by time: compact (12 sessions), standard (24), or extended
  (36+), depending on available kits and expected depth.

## Designed for grades 7, 8, and 9

The program uses the same projects as a base, but the expectation changes by grade: grade 7
measures guided comprehension and execution; grade 8 measures analysis, adjustment, and debugging;
grade 9 measures original design, technical documentation, and community impact.

<BasicoAlignment />

## Team work profiles

Organize teams with three rotating profiles:

- **Builder** — assembles the robot and compares the structure with the visual guide.
- **Organizer** — takes care of materials, coordinates timing, and verifies each step is closed out.
- **Programmer** — opens the base program, adjusts blocks, and explains how the code relates to the
  robot's behavior.

The recommendation is to rotate profiles during the module so everyone explores a different
responsibility.

## Curriculum framework

Beyond the **lower-secondary CNB**, the program lets you report evidence in **Mathematics**,
**Natural Sciences**, **Learning and Communication Technologies**, **Communication and Language,
Spanish**, and **Entrepreneurship for Productivity**. It also connects to international technology,
computer science, and engineering standards: **ISTE Standards for Students**, **2026 CSTA PK-12
Computer Science Standards**, and **NGSS MS-ETS1 Engineering Design**. Evidence shouldn't just be
"the robot worked" — it should include code, test data, a technical explanation, and design
improvement.
