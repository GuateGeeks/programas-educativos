# 02 · Delivery Bot

:::note For the teacher
Review the **Implementation** tab first (phases and concepts), prepare the **Resources** (base
program and guide), then close out planning with **CNB & Standards** and **Evaluation**. You can
rotate team roles — Builder, Organizer, Programmer — throughout the module.
:::

<Module id="m2">
  <Module.Question>
    How do we design a robot that safely delivers a package in a neighborhood, village, or school
    campus?
  </Module.Question>

  <Module.Context>
    The robot simulates a local GuateGeeks delivery service: bringing medicine, a spare part,
    books, or materials to the right person. It works with autonomous movement, subroutines,
    opening/closing a compartment, buttons, the hub display, and access logic.
  </Module.Context>

  <Module.Concepts items={[
    "Subroutines",
    "Opening and closing with a motor",
    "Route sequences",
    "Buttons and PIN",
    "Secure delivery design",
  ]} />

  <Module.Phase kind="act" label="Activate · 15 min" title="Robots in delivery services">
    Talk about food orders, packages, and safety. What real problems would a delivery robot face
    on the street?
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="exp" label="Explore · 25 min" title="Compartment and mechanism">
    Analyze the large motor, medium motors, lid, and crank mechanism. Manually test opening and
    closing without forcing parts.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="cre" label="Create · 60-90 min" title="Route with access control">
    Build the Delivery Bot. Create blocks to open, close, turn left, turn right, and move forward.
    Integrate a PIN sequence with buttons before opening.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Phase kind="ref" label="Reflect · 15 min" title="Safety and user experience">
    Each group explains how they'd avoid wrong deliveries and what they'd do if the robot
    encounters an obstacle.
  </Module.Phase>

  <Module.Cnb items={[
    "Mathematics: patterns, sequence, and comparing routes.",
    "Communication and Language: clear instructions and explaining rules.",
    "Civic Education: safety, responsibility, and community services.",
    "Productivity and Development: designing solutions for local needs.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Standards items={[
    "CSTA: procedures and events.",
    "ISTE: creative use of technology to solve problems.",
    "NGSS ETS: compare solutions against criteria and constraints.",
  ]} />

  <Module.Evaluation items={[
    "Breaks complex actions into subroutines.",
    "Integrates route and opening without manual intervention.",
    "Justifies the system's safety rules.",
  ]} />
</Module>
