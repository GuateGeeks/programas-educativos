## Why

GuateGeeks already has a strong educational asset in CiudadBots: a structured robotics curriculum, teacher guide, student view, downloadable resources, standards alignment, and final showcase. The current site proves the team can create high-quality educational content, but it still behaves mainly as a documentation hub. To turn this into a scalable edtech business, GuateGeeks needs a product system that packages programs, implementation support, physical equipment, evidence, and commercial materials into an institutional offer that schools, NGOs, companies, and education partners can understand and buy.

The immediate opportunity is practical: GuateGeeks already has a client, physical equipment, and a real program in the Constructores tier. Before creating more content or changing the site, the team needs a clear operating structure that connects product strategy, educational design, implementation, marketing, sales, and customer success. This change defines that structure so execution can proceed step by step instead of reacting module by module.

## What Changes

- Define a three-tier product ladder for GuateGeeks educational programs: Exploradores, Constructores, and Creadores.
- Position CiudadBots as the first real Constructores product and the anchor for institutional pilots.
- Define the institutional offer model: demo, paid pilot, semester program, annual program, and optional hardware/service bundles.
- Define how physical equipment supports delivery without becoming the only value proposition.
- Define the evidence system that makes learning visible to directors, teachers, parents, sponsors, and partner institutions.
- Define the commercial and communication assets needed to sell the program before October: landing, brochure, deck, demo flow, proposal template, case-study format, and WhatsApp/email scripts.
- Define a 90-day execution roadmap and task breakdown that can be applied in small increments.
- Keep fictitious/aspirational Exploradores and Creadores concepts clearly labeled as concept products until validated.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `guategeeks-product-system`: The overall product operating model for GuateGeeks as a program-based edtech company.
- `product-ladder`: The Exploradores, Constructores, and Creadores tier structure, including maturity, audience, promise, and expected outcomes.
- `ciudadbots-institutional-product`: CiudadBots packaged as an institutional product rather than only a teacher documentation site.
- `hardware-service-model`: Equipment options for owned kits, rental kits, recommended kits, and full-service implementation.
- `membership-model`: The future access model for public content, private institutional content, user roles, and subscription/licensing options.
- `teacher-onboarding`: The onboarding and enablement flow for teachers and coordinators.
- `evidence-and-showcase`: The system for rubrics, learning evidence, final reports, certificates, and public showcases.
- `institutional-sales`: The sales motion for schools, NGOs, companies, and sponsors.
- `marketing-communications`: Messaging, content, and promotion assets for product launch and client acquisition.
- `customer-success`: Renewal, support, feedback, and expansion practices after a pilot or program starts.

### Modified Capabilities

- `docs-platform`: The existing Docusaurus hub will later need product-facing entry points and calls to action, but this change first defines strategy and artifacts before modifying the site.
- `ciudadbots-overview-structure`: The overview may later gain commercial framing or a separate institutional landing page, while preserving the teacher guide.
- `student-mode`: The student view may later become part of a role-based membership experience, but remains static and public until access control is explicitly designed.

## Impact

- **Strategic scope:** Expands the project from "program documentation" into a product system spanning content, services, hardware, evidence, sales, and customer success.
- **Implementation sequence:** Prioritizes proposal/design/spec artifacts before code changes. Site edits should happen only after the product structure is agreed.
- **Commercial urgency:** Creates a path to sell pilots before October without waiting for a complete membership platform.
- **Content strategy:** Avoids creating many new programs before validating CiudadBots as an institutional product.
- **Operational model:** Treats physical equipment as an adoption accelerator and service layer, not the core product.
- **Risk management:** Keeps Exploradores and Creadores as conceptual packages until they are validated with real clients.
