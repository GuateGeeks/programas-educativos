## Purpose

Defines the future membership and access model for GuateGeeks educational programs while avoiding premature platform complexity.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Membership deferred until validation

GuateGeeks SHALL validate paid institutional demand before building a full authentication, payment, or LMS platform.

#### Scenario: Team considers private accounts
- **WHEN** the team proposes user accounts or payments
- **THEN** the decision is evaluated against current pilot sales, implementation needs, and renewal evidence.

### Requirement: Public and private content distinction

The product system SHALL distinguish public marketing/sample content from institutional resources intended for paying clients.

#### Scenario: Website content is planned
- **WHEN** a new resource is added
- **THEN** the team decides whether it is public proof, public sample, institutional client material, or internal delivery material.

### Requirement: Manual membership allowed

Before automation, GuateGeeks MAY deliver institutional access through controlled links, PDFs, manual onboarding, shared drives, or client-specific instructions.

#### Scenario: First paying client starts
- **WHEN** a paying client starts before a private portal exists
- **THEN** GuateGeeks can still deliver content and support through manual access controls.
