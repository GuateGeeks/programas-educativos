## Purpose

Defines CiudadBots as GuateGeeks' first institutional Constructores product. CiudadBots must be packaged for buyers, not only presented as teacher documentation.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Institutional packaging

CiudadBots SHALL be packaged as an institutional robotics program with clear duration options, audiences, outcomes, deliverables, and implementation support.

#### Scenario: Director reviews CiudadBots
- **WHEN** a director reviews the CiudadBots offer
- **THEN** they understand the program duration, what students do, what teachers receive, and what evidence leadership gets.

### Requirement: Multiple implementation formats

CiudadBots SHALL support compact, pilot, semester, and annual formats.

#### Scenario: Institution has limited time
- **WHEN** a school only has a short window
- **THEN** GuateGeeks can offer a pilot or compact format without claiming it is the full program.

### Requirement: Stakeholder outcomes

CiudadBots SHALL define outcomes for at least four stakeholders: directors, teachers, students, and parents or sponsors.

#### Scenario: Sales deck explains value
- **WHEN** the CiudadBots deck is presented
- **THEN** it includes a value statement for each stakeholder group.

### Requirement: Teacher guide remains distinct from commercial landing

The teacher-facing CiudadBots guide SHALL remain focused on implementation, while a separate product-facing section or landing explains the institutional offer.

#### Scenario: Teacher opens module guide
- **WHEN** a teacher is preparing class
- **THEN** they are not distracted by sales-oriented copy.

#### Scenario: Buyer opens product page
- **WHEN** a buyer evaluates CiudadBots
- **THEN** they see outcomes, packages, evidence, and next steps rather than only classroom instructions.
