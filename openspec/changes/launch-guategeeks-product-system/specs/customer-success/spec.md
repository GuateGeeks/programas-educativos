## Purpose

Defines how GuateGeeks supports clients after a sale so pilots can become renewals and longer-term programs.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Client kickoff

Every paid pilot or program SHALL start with a client kickoff covering goals, schedule, roles, equipment, evidence, and communication channels.

#### Scenario: Pilot is approved
- **WHEN** a client approves a pilot
- **THEN** GuateGeeks runs a kickoff before delivery starts.

### Requirement: Feedback loop

Every pilot SHALL collect feedback from at least the teacher/coordinator and leadership contact.

#### Scenario: Pilot ends
- **WHEN** delivery is complete
- **THEN** GuateGeeks collects structured feedback before proposing the next step.

### Requirement: Renewal recommendation

Every completed pilot or program SHALL end with a recommendation: continue, expand, adjust, pause, or discontinue.

#### Scenario: Results are presented
- **WHEN** the final report is delivered
- **THEN** it includes a recommended next commercial and educational action.
