## Purpose

Defines how GuateGeeks enables teachers and coordinators to implement programs without needing to be robotics experts.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Teacher onboarding flow

Every paid pilot or program SHALL include a teacher onboarding flow before classroom implementation.

#### Scenario: Teacher starts a pilot
- **WHEN** a teacher is assigned to a GuateGeeks pilot
- **THEN** they receive orientation on goals, session flow, student roles, equipment use, evidence, and support channels.

### Requirement: Support touchpoints

Each implementation SHALL define support touchpoints before, during, and after delivery.

#### Scenario: Teacher has difficulty during implementation
- **WHEN** a teacher encounters a setup, classroom, or activity issue
- **THEN** they know how and when to contact GuateGeeks support.

### Requirement: Minimum teacher templates

GuateGeeks SHALL provide the minimum templates needed for implementation: session plan, materials checklist, student role guide, evidence checklist, and rubric.

#### Scenario: Teacher prepares a session
- **WHEN** a teacher prepares class
- **THEN** they can use templates without needing to design the experience from scratch.
