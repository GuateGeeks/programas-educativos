## Purpose

Defines GuateGeeks as a program-based edtech product system rather than a collection of technology workshops. This capability describes the operating model that connects educational programs, teacher support, physical equipment, evidence, sales, and customer success.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Product thesis

GuateGeeks SHALL position its educational offer as institutional technology programs ready to implement, with content, teacher guidance, student experiences, physical equipment options, measurable evidence, and final showcases.

#### Scenario: Director evaluates GuateGeeks
- **WHEN** a school director reviews the GuateGeeks offer
- **THEN** the director understands that GuateGeeks provides an implementable program system, not only isolated workshops or equipment.

### Requirement: Outcome-led positioning

GuateGeeks SHALL lead with institutional outcomes: differentiated learning, teacher enablement, visible student work, and evidence for leadership, parents, sponsors, or partners.

#### Scenario: Sales material is reviewed
- **WHEN** a sales asset introduces GuateGeeks
- **THEN** it explains what institutions gain before listing technologies such as robotics, AI, drones, VR, or 3D printing.

### Requirement: Program-plus-service model

GuateGeeks SHALL define each institutional offer as a combination of educational content, implementation support, hardware/service options, and evidence/reporting deliverables.

#### Scenario: A package is proposed
- **WHEN** GuateGeeks sends a pilot or program proposal
- **THEN** the proposal identifies content, service, equipment, evidence, and support components separately.

### Requirement: Incremental validation

GuateGeeks SHALL validate the product system through paid pilots before investing in full membership automation, authentication, or an LMS.

#### Scenario: A new capability is requested
- **WHEN** the team considers building a complex platform feature
- **THEN** it first checks whether the feature is required to sell, implement, or renew the next pilot.
