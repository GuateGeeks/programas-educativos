## Purpose

Defines the communication and promotion assets needed to sell GuateGeeks programs.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Outcome-first messaging

Marketing communication SHALL lead with institutional outcomes and teacher support before listing technologies.

#### Scenario: Prospect reads first screen
- **WHEN** a prospect reads the first screen of a landing page or brochure
- **THEN** they understand the educational and institutional value before seeing a technology list.

### Requirement: Required launch assets

Before a sales push, GuateGeeks SHALL prepare a one-page brochure, sales deck, demo agenda, outreach scripts, pilot proposal template, evidence report template, and case-study template.

#### Scenario: Sales sprint begins
- **WHEN** the team begins outreach
- **THEN** the team has the minimum assets needed to explain, demo, propose, and follow up.

### Requirement: Website product structure

The public website SHALL eventually present the product ladder, CiudadBots institutional offer, hardware options, evidence promise, and call to action.

#### Scenario: Buyer visits the hub
- **WHEN** a buyer lands on the hub
- **THEN** they can understand the offer and request a demo or pilot without needing to navigate teacher documentation first.
