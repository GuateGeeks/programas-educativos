## Purpose

Defines the institutional sales motion for GuateGeeks educational programs.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Founder-led pilot sales

Initial sales SHALL focus on founder-led discovery, demo, pilot proposal, pilot execution, evidence review, and expansion.

#### Scenario: New target institution is contacted
- **WHEN** GuateGeeks contacts a target institution
- **THEN** the goal is to book discovery and move toward a paid pilot, not immediately sell a full platform.

### Requirement: Target segment selection

The team SHALL select a primary target segment for the next 90 days.

#### Scenario: Marketing content is created
- **WHEN** GuateGeeks writes outreach or landing copy
- **THEN** the copy reflects the selected primary segment rather than speaking generically to everyone.

### Requirement: Proposal template

GuateGeeks SHALL maintain a reusable pilot proposal template covering problem, objectives, package, schedule, equipment model, deliverables, investment, and next steps.

#### Scenario: Discovery call goes well
- **WHEN** a prospect asks for a proposal
- **THEN** GuateGeeks can send a consistent pilot proposal quickly.

### Requirement: Post-pilot expansion

Every pilot SHALL include a post-pilot review step that recommends renewal, semester program, annual program, or no-go.

#### Scenario: Pilot evidence is reviewed
- **WHEN** GuateGeeks presents results
- **THEN** the next commercial step is explicit.
