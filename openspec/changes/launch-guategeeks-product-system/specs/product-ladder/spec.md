## Purpose

Defines the GuateGeeks product ladder: Exploradores, Constructores, and Creadores. The ladder helps institutions start at the right level and gives GuateGeeks a clear path from demo to pilot to deeper programs.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Three-tier ladder

The product system SHALL include three program tiers: Exploradores, Constructores, and Creadores.

#### Scenario: Institution asks where to start
- **WHEN** an institution describes its maturity, budget, and goals
- **THEN** GuateGeeks can recommend one of the three tiers with a clear rationale.

### Requirement: Exploradores entry tier

Exploradores SHALL be defined as a low-risk introductory tier for institutions that want to experience applied technology before committing to a full program.

#### Scenario: School wants a first experience
- **WHEN** a school is curious but not ready for a semester program
- **THEN** GuateGeeks can offer a 4-6 session Exploradores concept with simple outputs and evidence.

### Requirement: Constructores core tier

Constructores SHALL be defined as the structured program tier, with CiudadBots as the first real product.

#### Scenario: School wants a formal robotics program
- **WHEN** a school wants a structured robotics or STEAM implementation
- **THEN** GuateGeeks can offer CiudadBots as a Constructores program with sessions, resources, rubrics, and showcase.

### Requirement: Creadores premium tier

Creadores SHALL be defined as the advanced innovation tier where students design original solutions connected to real needs.

#### Scenario: School wants differentiation
- **WHEN** a school wants a premium innovation or capstone experience
- **THEN** GuateGeeks can describe Creadores as a future program model focused on original prototypes, documentation, and public presentation.

### Requirement: Maturity labeling

Each tier SHALL indicate whether it is real, partial, conceptual, or future.

#### Scenario: Sales team presents the ladder
- **WHEN** GuateGeeks shows the three levels to a client
- **THEN** the presentation does not imply that conceptual tiers are fully built.
