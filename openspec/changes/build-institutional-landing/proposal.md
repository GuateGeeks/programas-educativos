## Why

The current GuateGeeks programs homepage works as a documentation hub, but it does not yet sell the institutional value of GuateGeeks. A director, coordinator, sponsor, or partner can see CiudadBots, but they do not immediately understand the product ladder, the implementation support, the hardware options, the evidence promise, or how to request a pilot.

GuateGeeks needs a public landing experience that explains the offer before visitors enter teacher documentation. This is the first implementation change after approving the broader product system.

## What Changes

- Rework the public homepage into an institutional product landing for GuateGeeks educational programs.
- Present the core value proposition: programs ready for classroom implementation with teacher support, evidence, and optional equipment.
- Add the three-tier product ladder: Exploradores, Constructores, Creadores.
- Position CiudadBots as the featured Constructores product and current anchor offer.
- Explain package paths: demo, paid pilot, semester program, annual program.
- Explain equipment options at a high level.
- Explain evidence and showcase outcomes.
- Add clear calls to action for demo/pilot contact.
- Preserve existing CiudadBots teacher guide and student view routes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `institutional-landing`: Public product-facing landing page for buyers and partners.

### Modified Capabilities

- `docs-platform`: The homepage shifts from a simple documentation hub entry into a commercial/product landing while preserving Docusaurus hub navigation.
- `marketing-communications`: The site becomes the first public proof point for GuateGeeks' outcome-first messaging.
- `ciudadbots-institutional-product`: CiudadBots gains buyer-facing positioning before visitors enter the teacher guide.

## Impact

- **User experience:** Buyers get a clearer first impression and call to action.
- **Content:** Homepage copy and layout expand significantly.
- **Scope control:** No authentication, pricing automation, CRM, payments, or private membership portal in this change.
- **Risk:** The page must not overclaim Exploradores or Creadores as fully built; they should be labeled as pathways or conceptual tiers where appropriate.
