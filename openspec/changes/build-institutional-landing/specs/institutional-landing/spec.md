## Purpose

Defines the public buyer-facing homepage for GuateGeeks educational programs.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Institutional value proposition

The homepage SHALL present GuateGeeks as a provider of ready-to-implement technology programs with teacher support, student experiences, physical equipment options, and visible evidence.

#### Scenario: Buyer lands on homepage
- **WHEN** a director, coordinator, sponsor, or partner opens the homepage
- **THEN** they understand the institutional value before entering the teacher guide.

### Requirement: Product ladder presentation

The homepage SHALL present Exploradores, Constructores, and Creadores as the GuateGeeks product ladder.

#### Scenario: Buyer compares pathways
- **WHEN** a buyer reads the product ladder
- **THEN** they understand which pathway is introductory, structured, or advanced.

### Requirement: Maturity transparency

The homepage SHALL label or phrase Exploradores and Creadores in a way that avoids implying they are as complete as CiudadBots if they are still conceptual.

#### Scenario: Buyer sees future pathways
- **WHEN** a buyer sees Exploradores or Creadores
- **THEN** they understand them as pathways or program concepts rather than fully published products.

### Requirement: CiudadBots featured product

The homepage SHALL feature CiudadBots as the current Constructores anchor product and link to the existing teacher guide.

#### Scenario: Buyer wants proof
- **WHEN** a buyer clicks into CiudadBots
- **THEN** they can inspect the real guide, modules, and student experience.

### Requirement: Implementation and evidence explanation

The homepage SHALL explain how implementation works and what evidence the institution receives.

#### Scenario: Buyer asks what happens after purchase
- **WHEN** they read the homepage
- **THEN** they understand onboarding, sessions, evidence capture, showcase, and next-step recommendation.

### Requirement: CTA to demo or pilot

The homepage SHALL include a clear call to action for requesting a demo or pilot.

#### Scenario: Buyer is interested
- **WHEN** a buyer reaches the CTA
- **THEN** they have a clear contact path to start a conversation.
