## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: CNB curriculum alignment is fully documented
The system SHALL include complete CNB (Curriculum Nacional Base) mappings for each module, showing connections to current Ciclo Básico areas including `Matemática`, `Ciencias Naturales`, `Tecnologías del Aprendizaje y la Comunicación`, `Comunicación y Lenguaje, Idioma Español`, `Emprendimiento para la Productividad`, and optionally `Ciencias Sociales, Formación Ciudadana e Interculturalidad` or `Educación Artística` where applicable.

Each mapping SHALL connect the area to module-specific evidence rather than only listing the area name.

#### Scenario: CNB mappings are complete
- **WHEN** viewing a module's curriculum mappings
- **THEN** at least 4 CNB areas are mapped with specific curriculum connections and observable module evidence

### Requirement: Standards references are comprehensive
The system SHALL include standards references for ISTE Standards for Students, 2026 CSTA PK-12 Computer Science Standards at the middle-school level, and NGSS MS-ETS1 Engineering Design. Module references SHALL use current framework names and SHALL be specific enough to connect standards to module evidence.

#### Scenario: Standards are listed for a module
- **WHEN** viewing a module's standards section
- **THEN** references include ISTE, 2026 CSTA PK-12 middle-school framing, and NGSS MS-ETS1 where applicable

### Requirement: Evaluation criteria are specific and measurable
The system SHALL include evaluation criteria (rubric items) for each module that are specific, measurable, and actionable for teachers assessing student work. Criteria SHALL name observable student behaviors or deliverables such as robot function, program reasoning, debugging, measurement/data use, design iteration, safety constraints, collaboration, and communication.

#### Scenario: Evaluation criteria are provided
- **WHEN** viewing a module's evaluation section
- **THEN** at least 3 criteria are listed, each describing observable student behaviors or deliverables
